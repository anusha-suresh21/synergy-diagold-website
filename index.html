Who We Are

Trade with integrity, or not at all.

Precious metals move fast, and they move quietly — which is exactly why the market needs counterparties who slow down long enough to ask the right questions. Synergy Diagold FZCO was built on that premise.

We are a Dubai-based trading house engaged in the buying, selling and cross-border movement of gold and other precious metals. Behind every transaction sits a genuine commitment to verifying who we work with, understanding where value comes from, and keeping clean records of how it moves.

Synergy Diagold FZCO trades gold, silver, platinum and palladium from Dubai, where every counterparty is verified before a single gram changes hands.

What We Trade

Every metal we offer is refined to internationally recognised purity standards and delivered in standard bar formats suited to institutional, dealer and industrial buyers.

Why Synergy Diagold

What sets us apart.

Diligence-first trading, done by people who know the market and answer their own phones.

Trusted, Hands-On Expertise

Our leadership brings direct, practical experience in the bullion trade, not just an interest in it — deep market insight across gold, silver, platinum and palladium that shows up in how realistically we price, and how quickly we can move once terms are agreed.

A Reach Built on Trusted Partners

We work with clients across the UAE and beyond, backed by transport and storage partners we've properly vetted ourselves. And when you call, you reach a person — no call centres, no ticket queues.

Verified Counterparties, Every Time

We'd rather lose a fast deal than take on a counterparty we don't properly understand. That approach costs us some speed occasionally — it saves us, and our clients, far more in the long run.

Transparent, Market-Linked Pricing

Pricing on every trade reflects the market, not a marked-up version of it — the same straightforward structure whether it's a single transaction or an ongoing relationship.

Built for Institutional Volumes

Supply arrangements are scaled to fit the buyer — from a single kilo bar to recurring institutional demand — built around your volume and timing rather than a fixed catalogue.

Documented at Every Step

Every service we provide leaves a clear paper trail, coordinated only with logistics and storage partners we trust — useful for our clients' own record-keeping as much as for ours.
