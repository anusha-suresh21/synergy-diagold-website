How It Works

Precious Metals Trading

We buy and sell gold, silver, platinum and palladium in kilo and large bar formats, working to pricing that reflects the market rather than a marked-up version of it. Trades are scaled to fit the buyer, from single transactions to larger recurring volumes.

  • Kilo and large bar formats across gold, silver, platinum and palladium
  • Transparent, market-linked pricing on every trade
  • Capacity for high-volume, recurring trading relationships

Bullion Supply

For buyers who need a steady, dependable supply rather than a single transaction, we coordinate consistent access to investment-grade bullion — 999.9 fine gold, 999.0 fine silver and 999.5 fine platinum — suited to vault storage, reserve strategy, resale or industrial use.

  • Internationally recognised purity standards, with full traceability
  • Supply arrangements built around your volume and timing, not a fixed catalogue
  • Suited to vault storage, reserve holding, resale and industrial requirements

Sourcing & Procurement Support

When a client is looking for a supplier or buyer we haven't worked with before, we take the time to find and properly check one — supporting treasury teams with demand planning and supplier coordination — before making an introduction or agreeing terms on a client's behalf.

  • Tailored sourcing for institutions seeking long-term metal access
  • Full background checks on any new counterparty before an introduction is made
  • Support for procurement and demand planning where metal is needed on a schedule

Secure Movement & Delivery Coordination

We coordinate secured storage, handling and delivery of bullion through trusted logistics and vaulting partners, with armoured transport and full documentation support at every step.

  • Coordination with vetted, insured, armoured transport providers
  • Secure vaulting and storage arranged where required
  • Clear documentation and paper trail at every step of a delivery
Why Synergy Diagold

What sets our service apart.

Trusted, Hands-On Expertise

Our leadership brings direct, practical experience in the bullion trade, not just an interest in it — deep market insight across gold, silver, platinum and palladium that shows up in how realistically we price, and how quickly we can move once terms are agreed.

A Reach Built on Trusted Partners

We work with clients across the UAE and beyond, backed by transport and storage partners we've properly vetted ourselves. And when you call, you reach a person — no call centres, no ticket queues.

Verified Counterparties, Every Time

Transparent, Market-Linked Pricing

Built for Institutional Volumes

Documented at Every Step
