# Image Prompts — Synergy Diagold FZCO

Style anchor used across every prompt: navy/ink background (#0B1F33 family), muted-gold
lighting (#C89B3C family), macro product photography, private-bank/assay-office mood —
not a bright retail jewellery shot. No people, no logos, no text overlays, no watermarks.

---

## 1. hero-bullion.jpg
**Use:** Homepage hero background (behind the headline, right-hand side, dark navy overlay applied in CSS)
**Aspect:** 16:9, landscape, 2400x1350 or larger

> Macro photograph of a single large stamped gold bullion bar resting at a diagonal on a
> matte dark navy-charcoal surface, shot close-up so the fine stamped fineness marks and
> serial-number engraving are sharp and legible. Single directional light source from the
> upper left, low and raking, producing a narrow bright highlight along the bar's edge and
> deep shadow elsewhere in frame — dramatic, restrained, editorial. Background falls to
> near-black navy in soft gradient, no props, no other objects, no hands, no text overlays,
> no logos. Photographed on a Hasselblad medium-format look, shallow depth of field,
// slight film grain, cool color temperature with warm gold highlight. Mood: private vault,
> not retail jewellery counter.

## 2. gold-bar.jpg
**Use:** Metals & Certification page — Gold card
**Aspect:** 4:3 or 1:1, 1600x1600

> Macro product photograph of a single 999.9 fine gold bullion bar, fineness stamp and
> serial number crisply visible, resting on a dark navy slate surface. Soft single-source
> raking light from the left casting a warm gold highlight along the top edge, background
> falling into near-black shadow. No other objects in frame, no hands, no text overlays, no
> logos, no coins. Sharp macro focus on the stamped face, extremely shallow depth of field
> on the background. Institutional, restrained, editorial product photography — not a bright
> retail or stock-photo look.

**Use:** Metals & Certification page — Platinum Group (Platinum / Palladium / Rhodium) card

> Macro product photograph of two small polished silvery-white precious-metal bars (platinum

## Negative guidance (apply to all four)
Avoid: bright white studio backgrounds, piles/stacks of many coins, hands holding bars,
handshake imagery, vault doors, generic "wealth" stock-photo staging, visible logos or
text/numerals other than the bar's own stamped marks, cartoonish or overly saturated
rendering, jewellery-store retail lighting.
