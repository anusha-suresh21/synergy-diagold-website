About Us

Who We Are

About Synergy Diagold FZCO

Synergy Diagold FZCO is a Dubai-based trading company engaged in the buying, selling and cross-border movement of gold and other precious metals. We are based in Dubai Airport Free Zone, a location chosen for its connection to Dubai's logistics infrastructure and its standing as an established base for precious metals trade.

The precious metals trade carries a reputation problem it has largely earned: opaque sourcing, informal deals, and counterparties nobody has properly checked. Synergy Diagold was built to be the opposite of that — a trading house where the paperwork is as solid as the metal. We're a young company, but our approach to how we do business isn't improvised. We check who we deal with, we ask where value comes from, and we hold ourselves to that standard whether anyone is watching or not.

Our Mission

Reliable access, built on trust.

To provide our trading partners with reliable, transparent access to gold and other precious metals, underpinned by careful verification, responsible sourcing, and consistently honest dealing at every stage of every transaction.

Our Vision

Dubai's most trusted trading house.

To be recognised as one of Dubai's most trusted precious metals trading houses — a partner that institutions, dealers and businesses choose because integrity is treated as being every bit as important as price.

Our Values

Integrity, applied consistently.

Three things we don't compromise on: integrity in how we verify and document every relationship, a willingness to keep improving how we work rather than defaulting to habit, and a standard of execution our partners can rely on, trade after trade.

Managing Director & Founder

Mr. Shitiz Garg

Mr. Shitiz Garg is the Managing Director and Founder of Synergy Diagold FZCO, bringing over nine years of experience in high-volume gold bullion trading, business strategy and financial investments to the company's leadership. He holds overall responsibility for Synergy Diagold's strategic direction and for the standards it holds itself to in every transaction.

His professional background extends beyond precious metals into petroleum, real estate and general trading, shaped in part by a family business legacy with longstanding interests in commodities, manufacturing and real estate. That breadth of experience — across sourcing, counterparties, settlement, and the operational realities of moving metal at scale — is what shaped Synergy Diagold's founding principle: verify first, trade second.

Under his leadership, Synergy Diagold has been built to check who it works with from day one, rather than treating that as something to sort out later — a discipline he applies personally to every significant business relationship the company enters.

How We Work

The discipline behind every relationship.

Know Who We're Dealing With

Every new business relationship starts with a conversation and a proper look at who we'd be working with — what the business does, who owns it, and how it operates — before any transaction is discussed further.

A Tailored Approach to Risk

Some counterparties need more attention than others, and we adjust accordingly rather than applying a single fixed process to everyone.

Rigorous Sourcing Standards

We look at where the metal we handle actually comes from, and we're comfortable turning down business we can't be confident about.

Accurate, Transparent Record-Keeping

Clear, accurate records of who we deal with and what we agree to — not because a form requires it, but because it's how a trading relationship should be run.
