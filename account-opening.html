What We Need From You

Required documentation.

Every new counterparty goes through the same onboarding standard, regardless of size — it's the same due diligence discipline behind everything else we do.

  • Valid Trade License
  • Certificate of Incorporation
  • Memorandum & Articles of Association
  • Tax Registration Certificate
  • Shareholders Register
  • Proof of physical address in country of incorporation
  • Proof of physical address in UAE (if applicable)
  • Official company email address
  • Phone number
  • Company website (if available)
  • Shareholders & beneficial owners
  • Partners
  • Authorised signatories & representatives

If one individual fits more than one role above, their details only need to be provided once.
